On Praderas we use **tags** as categories: they stay stable in YAML, tie into search, and link to the classic tag listing (`/tags`).

This page is the **topic map**: short blurbs, approximate counts, and one-click access to filtered lists. Tag labels are still shared with the Spanish site; counts on this page reflect **English posts only** under `blog/en/`.
